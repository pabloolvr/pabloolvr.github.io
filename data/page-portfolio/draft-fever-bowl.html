Draft Fever Bowl

Draft Fever Bowl is an indie American football management simulation game for PC. Developed in Unity at Blue Gravity Studios

In this game I worked writing code on the Match Engine UI:
  • • Scoring UI panels (touchdowns, field goals, safeties, extra points and 2-point conversions)
  • • Scoring Drive panel
  • • Mini, Micro and Constant player highlights
  • • Player highlight circle and ball carrier indicators
  • • Scoreboard and scouting clipboard feedback
