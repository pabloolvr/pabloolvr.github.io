PIX Football Manager

PIX Football Manager is a nostalgic, casual football management simulator focused on scouting. With no visible player stats, users must spot talent themselves. Developed in Unity for PC at Blue Gravity Studios

In this game I worked writing code on the football match simulation using Photon Quantum:
  • • Goalkeeper AI using Behavior Trees
    • • Positioning and off-ball behavior
    • • Dives, smothers and cross interventions
    • • Penalty reactions and penalty shootouts
    • • Distribution (passes, long balls, punts and goal kicks)
  • • Injury System, affected by field conditions
  • • Match Rating and Player Statistics Systems
  • • Narrator and crowd reactions, SFX and Audio Settings
  • • Localization of the match simulation UI
  • • Pre-game, pause and match event UI
  • • Training drills and performance optimization
